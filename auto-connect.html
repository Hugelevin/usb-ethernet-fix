USB Ethernet Fix for TV

Auto-connect after restart

After every TV restart, and sometimes after standby, Android asks again for USB access. Auto-connect presses OK on that prompt for you, so the cable connects by itself.

What it does, and what it doesn't

  • It only looks at the TV's USB prompts, and only for a short time after the app has asked for USB access.
  • It presses OK on the prompt for this app's adapter. Nothing else.
  • It doesn't read what's on screen in other apps and doesn't collect or send anything.

Android lists it as an accessibility service, because that's the only way an app may press a button on a system prompt. You'll see it as USB Ethernet Fix: auto-connect after restart.

Turn it on

  1. In the app, open Settings and select Auto-connect after restart. The TV's accessibility settings open.
  2. Select USB Ethernet Fix: auto-connect after restart and turn it on.
  3. Confirm with OK, then press Back to return to the app. Settings shows On.

Turn it off

Same place: Settings, Auto-connect after restart, then turn it off in the TV's accessibility settings. The app keeps working. You'll just need to press OK on the USB prompt yourself after each restart.
