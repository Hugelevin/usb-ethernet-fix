USB Ethernet Fix for TV

Troubleshooting

"Wi-Fi isn't connected" and downloads are slow

When Wi-Fi is switched off while the TV is running, Android limits each download to about 10 Mbps, even over the cable. Two ways out:

  • Keep Wi-Fi connected. Your traffic still goes over the cable.
  • Or switch Wi-Fi off and restart the TV once. After the restart Android keeps full speed with Wi-Fi off.

The speed test

  • The test downloads from a public speed test service for a few seconds, several downloads at once, and uses up to about 250 MB.
  • With the app off it measures what the TV uses instead (usually Wi-Fi), so you can compare.
  • "Test server is busy. Try again in a few minutes." means the test service is limiting tests from your connection after several in a row. Wait, then try again.
  • "Couldn't reach the server" means the test couldn't connect at all. Check that the TV has internet.

Slower than expected

  • Check the cable speed on the main screen. It should say 1000 Mbps cable. If it says 100, try another network cable or router port.
  • In Settings, Full speed is the fastest mode if your adapter supports it.
  • One single download is often limited to about 200 Mbps by Android while Wi-Fi is connected. Apps that use several connections (most streaming apps) get more.

It doesn't reconnect after standby or a restart

Turn on Auto-connect after restart. Without it, the app waits until someone presses OK on the USB prompt.

Reporting a problem

  1. In the app, open Settings, then About and support.
  2. Scan the Diagnostics summary QR code with your phone. It contains the app version, TV, adapter, mode and the last error. Nothing else.
  3. Open a new issue, paste the text, and describe what happened.
