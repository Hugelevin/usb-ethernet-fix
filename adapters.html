USB Ethernet Fix for TV

Supported adapters

The app has two connection modes. You choose one in Settings.

  • Full speed: talks to the adapter's Realtek chip directly. The fastest mode, for Realtek adapters only.
  • Compatible: uses the standard USB Ethernet mode that most adapters have. Works more widely, a bit slower.

If you choose Full speed and the adapter can't use it, the app switches to Compatible by itself and tells you in Settings.

What you need

  • A free USB port on the TV (USB 3 gives the best speed).
  • A network cable from the adapter to your router.
